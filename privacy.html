Privacy policy

Effective 27 November 2026

Nova Sphere publishes parts of free, open-licence films on its own Instagram account (@nova_spheres) and YouTube channel (@NovaSphereOnline). This policy explains what the Nova Sphere publishing tool (the "app") does with data.

Who the app is for

The app is used only by Nova Sphere's owner, to post Nova Sphere's own videos to Nova Sphere's own accounts. It is not offered to the public, and it never signs in anyone else.

What the app accesses

  • YouTube (Google sign-in): permission to upload videos (youtube.upload) and to read the channel's own details (youtube.readonly), such as its name, its uploaded Shorts and their view, like and comment counts. The app does not read email, contacts, files, watch history or any other Google data.
  • Instagram (Meta login): permission to publish reels to @nova_spheres and to read that account's own post numbers (views, reach).

What is stored, and where

  • The sign-in tokens, encrypted, on the owner's own computer. They are never sent anywhere except to Google or Meta when the app talks to them.
  • The videos, their covers, captions, posting times and post numbers (for example "38 views after 24 hours"), on the same computer, to choose good posting times.

What is not done

  • No data is sold, rented or shared with anyone.
  • No data is used for advertising.
  • No data about viewers is collected. This website sets no cookies and has no analytics or trackers.

Google API Services

Nova Sphere's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube data is handled under the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Keeping and deleting data

Sign-in tokens are kept until they are replaced or the access is removed. Access can be removed at any time in the Google Account (Security › Third-party access) or in Instagram (Settings › Apps and websites); the app then stops working until signed in again. Post records are kept as long as the posts exist; ask below to have them deleted.

Contact

Questions about this policy: novasphereonline@gmail.com.

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date.
